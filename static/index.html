2. Categories of Data, Purposes and Legal Bases

  • Discord Account identifier (Snowflake ID) — for authorisation, linking playlists, “Liked” marks and history, and counting skip votes. Basis: performance of the Terms of Service.
  • Discord display name and avatar — obtained through Discord OAuth2 / the Activity SDK to show the profile in the interface, the list of listeners in a voice room and the authorship of shared playlists. Basis: performance of the Terms; legitimate interest in operating the interface.
  • User content — playlist titles, track lists, “Liked” marks, listening history (title, artist, link, artwork, duration, source). Basis: performance of the Terms.

  • Access-restriction data — identifier, name where available, reason and date of a measure applied for a breach of the Terms. Basis: legitimate interest in security and protection of the Service.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser and device information, time and parameters of requests (see Section 5). Basis: legitimate interest in security and stability.

3. Data That Is Not Processed

  • Discord account passwords, e-mail addresses, telephone numbers, payment or banking details;
  • the content of direct messages and text-channel messages (other than commands addressed to the bot);
  • data for advertising profiling; no advertising or analytics trackers are used in the Service;
  • special categories of personal data.

4. Voice Channels: No Recording

Speech is not recorded. The bot operates for audio output only. The Service does not receive, record, analyse or store the voices and conversations of voice-channel participants.

5. Technical Logs and the Administrator Action Log

For diagnostics, abuse prevention and incident investigation the Service keeps technical event logs (a size-limited buffer in memory). The administrator action log, which records sign-ins to the administration panel and state-changing operations (time, administrator identifier, IP address, type of action, result, parameters without secrets), is stored in a file on the Service’s server. Secret data (passwords, codes, tokens, cookies) is not written to the log. Only authorised administrators have access to the logs.

6. Local Storage and External Resources

The browser or Discord Activity stores only interface settings and player state: volume, language, theme, repeat mode, side-menu state, profile cache, the latest queue and playback history. This data is kept on the User’s device and can be removed by clearing site data.

To render the interface the User’s browser may contact external resources (Google Fonts, Font Awesome icons, libraries on jsDelivr, unpkg, cdnjs and esm.sh, the Discord Embedded App SDK, and the artwork CDNs of YouTube and SoundCloud). In doing so the relevant operators receive technical data (in particular the IP address and browser information) under their own privacy policies.

  • The Administration does not sell data and does not share it with third parties for marketing purposes;
  • data is processed using the infrastructure of the hosting provider and the Discord platform, and playback requests are sent to third-party platforms (YouTube Music, SoundCloud), which act independently as data controllers;
  • data may be disclosed on a reasoned request of competent authorities in accordance with law, and to protect the rights and security of the Administration and users.

8. Shared Playlists and Public Display

Playlists published in the shared (“user playlists”) section may be shown to other users of the Service together with the title, track list, and the author’s display name and avatar. The User may delete their playlist at any time.
